Named administrative coastal base lines for NMEFC forecast-area display

Mainland coverage: 62 coastal prefectures, directly administered counties/cities
and municipalities across 11 coastal provinces/municipalities, plus Hong Kong
and Macao. The earlier 17 Taiwan Province coastlines are retained.
Mainland marine outlines use the app's unchanged cn.province.topo.json, partitioned
using its existing city-boundary GeoJSON. Names and provinces must both match.
No inland provincial/city boundaries are drawn. Mainland coverage excludes distant
South China Sea decorative/sea features; they are not treated as coastal cities.
Mainland source hashes, city alignment and estuary endpoint reference information:
nmefc.named-coasts.provenance.json (mainland).
Detailed provenance: docs/nmefc_mainland_coasts_20261021.md.

Source: pinned TOWN_MOI_1120317 official township boundary package
URL: https://maps.nlsc.gov.tw/download/鄉鎮市區界線(TWD97經緯度).zip
Original ZIP SHA256: e028e5a750eee48cf7913330655e5e5c5bb1f176868fbd0afdfc661fca60557c
Build: tool/region_data/build_nmefc_coast_assets.py

Original package bytes remain unchanged in references/region_data/official.
These lines are derived administrative outer shores, not NMEFC-issued warning
boundaries or inundation limits. Inland administrative borders are excluded.
Only exactly matched named regions are activated; this is not a global NMEFC
forecast-area boundary database.
The user-selected display policy matches full county/city names and unambiguous
abbreviations at that level: 花莲 selects 花莲县, 宜兰 selects 宜兰县.
Ambiguous county/city aliases (such as 新竹) are not resolved by choosing the
first feature. Directional sectors such as 台东北 are not expanded.
Source warning colours and status come from the original forecasts array.
Generation method, coverage and output hash: nmefc.named-coasts.provenance.json.
