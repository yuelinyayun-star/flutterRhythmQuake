This is an ExpTech coastal base map, not a CWA-issued warning polygon or
an inundation prediction. Its AREANAME keys correspond to CWA's six named
coastal warning zones. Only zones named in the received CWA report are
drawn; warning status and colors come from that report, not this asset.
CWA zone definitions:
https://scweb.cwa.gov.tw/zh-tw/guidance/protection/228
