17 provincial/municipal units. The original downloaded source is retained
byte-for-byte. UTM-K (KGD2002, EPSG:5179) coordinates are converted to EPSG:4326
with offline pyproj 3.7.2, always_xy. All source rings and vertices are used,
with no simplification or buffers; existing CWR1 stores Float32 coordinates.
Chinese display aliases are separate from original Korean fields.

Two original Gyeongbuk polygon parts describing Dokdo/Takeshima are preserved
as one separate geographic-name feature, without Korean/Japanese ownership.
No marine, territorial sea or EEZ boundaries are generated from land polygons.
KMA's reference-place/direction/distance descriptions do not provide such
polygon boundaries; the existing FE sea-name query remains in use.

See korea_land_regions.provenance.json and tool/region_data/README.md.
