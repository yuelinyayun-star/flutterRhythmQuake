The township names, reference coordinates, areas and available site factors in
lib/core/cwa_prediction_towns.dart are a mechanical conversion of this public
dataset. Missing factors remain absent in the data. The unamplified model uses
a neutral factor of 1 for those reference points.

The application uses its existing Taiwan county boundaries and CWA palette.
No third-party proprietary client boundaries, colors or source code are bundled.
